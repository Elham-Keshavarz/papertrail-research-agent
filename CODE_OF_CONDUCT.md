# Code of Conduct

We aim to keep PaperTrail collaborative, respectful and welcoming. Offer specific technical feedback, consider other people's perspectives, and avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation and personal attacks.

Respect privacy, confidential information and the licenses of research papers and datasets. Never publish someone else's private research material, credentials or identifying information.

Maintainers may moderate or remove contributions and discussions violating these standards. To report harassment or safety issues, use a private channel offered by the repository maintainer. Do not disclose personal identifying details in public issues.
